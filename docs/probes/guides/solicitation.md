# Guide: solicitation

Version: v2. Read `common.md` first.

This guide has seven labels. A post can get several `yes`, or none.

Evidence for every label of this guide: required.

## `commercial_promo`

**Question.** Does the post promote something so that readers buy, book, download, subscribe to, watch or visit it?

**YES when both are true:**

1. The post presents a specific product, service, shop, app, course, paid event, publication, or the author's own content (a video, a stream, a podcast, a newsletter, an article, a store).
2. It pushes readers toward it: a price, a discount, "buy now", "order today", "available at", "link below", "out now", "check out my …", "book your seat".

**NO when:**

- the post is an opinion or a story about a product, with no offer;
- the post is news about a company;
- the post is a job advert.

**Examples.**

- YES: "New drop! 30% off all hoodies this weekend, link below" → `30% off all hoodies this weekend`
- YES: "My new video is out, check it out on my channel" → `check it out on my channel`
- NO: "Acme shares fell 4% after the earnings call" (news)
- NO: "This phone's battery is disappointing" (an opinion)

## `money_request`

**Question.** Does the post ask readers to give money?

**YES when:**

1. The post asks readers to send or give money: donate, support a fundraiser, tip, "help me pay rent", "send to my PayPal", "chip in", "pledge".

**NO when:**

- the post sells something (that is `commercial_promo`);
- the post reports a fundraiser and asks for nothing ("the charity raised one million").

**Examples.**

- YES: "Anything helps. Please donate to our fundraiser, link below" → `Please donate to our fundraiser`
- YES: "Rent is due Friday and I'm short. Cashapp in bio if you can spare" → `Cashapp in bio if you can spare`
- NO: "The appeal has raised £1m for flood victims" (a report)

## `gain_promise`

**Question.** Does the post offer readers a way to get money?

**YES when both are true:**

1. The post promises or dangles money, profit or a prize for the reader: investment returns, trading signals, crypto or token gains, betting tips, "earn X a day from home", "double your money", a cash giveaway, "you have won".
2. It presents this as something the reader can get, not only as the author's own result.

**NO when:**

- the post is market news ("Bitcoin up 5% today");
- the author reports their own gain and offers nothing ("I made 20% this year").

**Examples.**

- YES: "Earn $500 a day from your phone, no experience needed" → `Earn $500 a day from your phone`
- YES: "Giving $1,000 to five followers tonight" → `Giving $1,000 to five followers tonight`
- NO: "Markets closed higher on Friday" (news)

## `engagement_bait`

**Question.** Does the post ask for a like, a follow, a repost, a comment or a tag as a trade or a token?

**YES when both are true:**

1. The post asks for an engagement action: like, follow, repost, comment, reply, tag.
2. It either offers something in return (a prize, an entry in a draw, a follow back, a DM, a shout-out), or makes the action a token of agreement or identity ("like if you agree", "RT if you love your mum", "type AMEN").

**NO when:**

- the action is asked in order to spread a message, with no trade and no token ("please share, people need to know");
- the post only asks a question to start a conversation ("what do you think?").

**Examples.**

- YES: "Follow + RT to enter. Winner drawn Sunday" → `Follow + RT to enter`
- YES: "Like if you agree, ignore if you hate freedom" → `Like if you agree`
- NO: "Please share this, the family is still looking for him" (spreading a message)

## `off_platform_redirect`

**Question.** Does the post send readers to a private or off-platform channel?

**YES when:**

1. The post tells readers to continue through a private or off-platform channel: "DM me", "inbox me", a WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal or Snapchat contact, a phone number to call or text, an email address to write to, "link in bio", "link in my profile".

**NO when:**

- the post only gives a website link or names a website;
- the contact belongs to a public office or an emergency service;
- the post only mentions an app ("Telegram was down today").

**Examples.**

- YES: "Interested? DM me for details" → `DM me for details`
- YES: "WhatsApp +1 555 0147 to get started" → `WhatsApp +1 555 0147`
- NO: "Full article on our website" (a website)

## `adult_promo`

**Question.** Does the post promote sexual content or sexual services?

**YES when:**

1. The post offers or advertises sexual content or services: a subscription page for explicit content, "nudes", cam shows, escorting, sexting, an explicit invitation to get such content through a link, a profile or a message.

**NO when:**

- the post talks or jokes about sex and offers nothing;
- the post is news or education about sexuality.

**Examples.**

- YES: "New spicy content on my page tonight 🔞 link in bio" → `New spicy content on my page tonight`
- NO: "That film had far too many sex scenes" (an opinion)

## `testimonial`

**Question.** Does the author tell their own good experience with a named product, service or provider?

**YES when all are true:**

1. The post is in the first person ("I", "my", "we").
2. It tells the author's experience with a product, a service, a method, a professional, a platform or a scheme that is named: a brand, a product name, a person's name or an `@name`.
3. The experience is positive.

**NO when:**

- nothing is named ("love my new phone");
- the experience is negative or mixed;
- the praise is not a personal experience ("Acme makes great phones").

**Examples.**

- YES: "I lost everything to a scam until @recovery_pro got my funds back in two days" → `@recovery_pro got my funds back in two days`
- YES: "Been using the Acme X2 for a year and the battery is still perfect" → `Been using the Acme X2 for a year`
- NO: "Acme is the best phone brand out there" (no personal experience)
- NO: "My Acme X2 died after three months" (negative)
